How to read this table

Home and Away are how the fixture was listed, not proof of home advantage. Matches at a neutral ground are marked Neutral. Open a match to see whether it was a Test, whether it moved the rankings, and whether the ground was neutral. The winning side is shown in bolder type.

No matches fit all of those filters at once.

Loosen one of them — or that combination genuinely never happened.
